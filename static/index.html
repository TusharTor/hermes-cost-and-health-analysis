FinOps + Observability

Understand cost anomalies. Investigate resource health.

Ops Agent analyzes cloud resource costs to identify meaningful trends, deviations, spikes, drops, volatility, and anomalies. It then connects significant cost events with health signals from the affected resource to help determine whether unusual spending is associated with an underlying resource-health condition.

Start with the overall cost picture, select a resource to inspect its individual cost trend, and click a cost anomaly point to drill into health signals for that resource and day.
